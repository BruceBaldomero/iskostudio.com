Biggest opportunity: the page's own promise ("get back to running your business," "I read every one myself") is undercut right where it matters most — the contact form is the densest, most impersonal part of the page, and it's the last thing a visitor sees after 11 sections of otherwise careful design.

## Priority Issues

[P1] Accent color fails WCAG AA contrast in real usages
- Why it matters: Measured at 3.8-4.2:1 against a 4.5:1 requirement, in spots the accent is actually used for text. Contradicts CLAUDE.md's own stated rule.
- Fix: Darken the accent slightly for text-sized usage, or bump weight/size where used as body-scale text.
- Suggested command: /impeccable polish

[P1] Contact form shows ~13 fields/controls at once
- Why it matters: The page's one conversion action, reached after 11 sections, contradicts the form's own promise ("as much or as little as you've got"). The "optional details" block is labelled optional but fully expanded by default.
- Fix: Collapse "A few optional details" behind a disclosure toggle so the required path is name/email/message/submit.
- Suggested command: /impeccable distill

[P1] No inline, visible form error state
- Why it matters: Feedback relies entirely on the browser's native validation bubble, easy to miss on mobile Safari, especially since required fields aren't visually distinguished from the optional block beneath them.
- Fix: Add a lightweight inline error style and an aria-live="polite" status region near submit.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P2] Heading hierarchy skips a level + hover-only affordances
- Why it matters: h1 to h3 with no h2 is a real structural defect. Separately, review cards' "Read more" cue only appears on hover, giving touch/keyboard users no persistent signal the card expands.
- Fix: Insert the missing heading level or downgrade value-strip titles off the heading scale; make "Read more" visible at reduced opacity by default.
- Suggested command: /impeccable polish

[P2] Generic form-failure message with no safe retry
- Why it matters: "Something went wrong" doesn't distinguish network vs validation failure, no indication whether a partial submission went through.
- Fix: Differentiate failure types in the catch handler; preserve field values, offer a clear retry affordance.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

## Persona Red Flags

Jordan (first-timer): Reads pricing, then must cross-reference the FAQ accordion later to understand what the monthly fee covers — unresolved working-memory load. In the contact form, single-select "Biggest challenge" pills and multi-select "What do you need?" pills look visually identical.

Casey (mobile, distracted): On 375px, the feature work-card stacks a wide image, title, long description, 5 feature tags, and a 3-stat block before the next card. The Fix-Up band's CTA pre-selects a pill further down the page with no visible confirmation at the point of click.

Sam (accessibility-dependent): The comparison table's mobile row labels come from CSS ::before content, not reliably announced by screen readers. Review cards are role="button" with no aria-expanded or description of what activating them does.
